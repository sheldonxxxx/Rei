Weeknight Miso Salmon

This is the salmon I make when I have twenty minutes and no plan. The glaze caramelises under the grill while the rice finishes.

Serves 2 Prep 5 min Cook 12 min
ADVERTISEMENT: Try our meal kit, first box free

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets, about 150 g each
  • 2 tablespoons white miso
  • 1 tablespoon mirin
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • Spring onions and sesame seeds to serve

Method

  1. Heat the grill to high and line a tray with foil.
  2. Whisk the miso, mirin, soy sauce and honey into a thick paste.
  3. Spread the paste over the top of the fillets and grill for 8 to 10 minutes, until the glaze is dark at the edges.
  4. Rest for two minutes, then scatter with spring onions and sesame seeds.

Notes

White miso is milder than red miso. If you only have red miso, use half the amount and add an extra teaspoon of honey.
