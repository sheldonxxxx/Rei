Router keeps dropping Wi-Fi every evening

jules_k

Every evening around 8pm my router drops all wireless clients for a minute or two. Wired is fine. Any ideas?

netguy42

Probably channel congestion when your neighbours get home. What channel are you on?

jules_k

Auto, I think. It shows channel 6 right now.

mira_w

Check the system log for DFS events if you are on 5 GHz.

jules_k

Log shows "radar detected, switching channel" at 20:02 most days.

netguy42

That is it. Your router is hearing weather radar on a DFS channel and has to vacate. Pin the 5 GHz band to channel 36 or 44, which are not DFS channels, and the drops will stop.

jules_k

Pinned to 36 last night, no drop this evening. Thanks!
